Who We Are

Engineering Automation, End to End

NSK Automation is an engineering company based in Hosur, Tamil Nadu, specializing in the design and fabrication of automation lines and custom-built machinery. We serve customers across industries with equipment engineered around their exact process requirements.

Beyond building machines, we stand behind every installation - our team handles on-site setup and commissioning so equipment is running at full performance from day one.
